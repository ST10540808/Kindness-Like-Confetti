Volunteer

Make a difference with an act of kindness

Your time, skills and support can help us create a supportive community.

How you can help:

  • Help organize and run fundraising and community events.
  • Assist with preparing mentsrual products and care kits.
  • Get involved in initiatives that support girls and women in need

Full Time Volunteers

Full-time volunteers can assist with community outreach and organising events.

It is ideal for people who are able to volunteer consistently and take on regular resposibilities.

Part-Time Volunteers

Support our organisation while balancing other commitments.

Part time volunteers can help with fundraising and awareness campaigns.

It is ideal for people who have a few hours available each week or month.

Occasional Volunteers

Occasional volunteers can join us for special events and donation drives throughout the year.

It is ideal for people who want to contribute when their schedule allows.
