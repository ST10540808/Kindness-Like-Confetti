About Us

Spreading kindness, creating change.

Kindness Like Confetti is a South African non-profit organisation dedicated to supporting communities affected by period poverty, inequality and gender-based challenges.

Our Story

Where it all started

Kindness Like Confetti was founded in 2017 by Alyson Samson, who began by collecting menstrual products to support girls who could not afford them. What started as a simple act of kindness grew into an organisation focused on creating lasting change and supporting communities in need.

What we do

Our work

  • Fight Period Poverty
  • Provide Education
  • Support Communities

Our Programmes

  • Pad Princess

    • The Pad Princess programme provides sanitary products, reusable menstrual kits and menstrual health education to help people manage their periods with dignity and confidence.

  • Delicate Needs

    • Throught the Delicate Needs initiative, we focus on addressing essential needs while raising awareness around period poverty, gender based violence and inequality.

Our Mission

To spread kindness and create meaningful change by providing support, education and essential resources to communities affected by inequality and period poverty.

Our Vision

A future where everyone has access to the resources, education and support they need to live with dignity.
