## Introduction
Kindness Like Confetti is a non-profit south african organisation that encourages people to spread small acts of kindness in their everyday lives. The organisation aims to educate and uplift and empower individuals by providing practical support and resources to people in need. Its work focuses on issues such as period poverty, gender based violence and gender inequality. The organisation began as a simple initiative to collect menstrual products for girls who could not afford them and has since grown into a movement focused on supporting vulnerable communities. Overall, Kindness Like Confetti aims to create a more supportive society by responding to practical needs while also promoting compassion and hope.
