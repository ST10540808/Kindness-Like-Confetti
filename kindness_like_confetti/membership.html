Become a member

Be part of the movement

Join the Kindness Like Confetti community and help us create positive change. As a member, you can support our initiatives, participate in activities and connect with others who believe in building kinder, more equal communities.

Why you should become a member?

  • Make a Difference

    • Support initiatives that help address period poverty, inequality and community needs.

  • Get Involved

    • Take part in campaigns, fundraising activities, events and community projects.

  • Stay Connected

    • Receive updates about upcoming events, opportunities and ways to get involved.

  • Grow with Us

    • Meet like minded people and become part of a supportive community.

Membership Options:

Individual member

For people who want to support and participate in our work.

Student member

For students who want to volunteer, learn and contribute to community initiatives.

Organization/Corporate Member

For businesses and organizations interested in supporting or partnering with Kindess Like Confetti.
