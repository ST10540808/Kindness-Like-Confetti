Welcome To Kindness Like Confetti

Spreading kindness and creating positive change.

We are committed to supporting communitites affected by period poverty and inequality by providing menstrual products, education and meaningful support.

Who we are

Making a difference, one act of kindness at a time

Kindness Like Confetti is a South African non-profit organization working to support people affected by period poverty and inequality. We believe that something as simple as providing a menstrual product, sharing knowledge or giving your time can have a lasting impact.

PERIOD POVERTY IS MORE THAN JUST A PERIOD

Many people are not guaranteed menstrual products. The lack of affordable menstrual products and menstrual health education can affect confidence, education and everyday life.

This is where we come in

Kindness Like Confetti works to provide practical support while creating awareness and encouraging conversations around menstrual health and dignity.

Creating change through action

From providing menstrual products to creating educational opportunities, Kindness Like Confetti turns community support into meaningful action. Our initiatives focus on helping people access the resources they need while creating awareness around period poverty.

Our Goals

  • Increase access to menstrual products
  • Expand menstrual health education
  • Reach more schools and communities
  • Grow our volunteer network

KINDNESS STARTS WITH YOU.

A small act of kindness can make a big difference. Join us in creating a future where no one to miss out because of their period.
